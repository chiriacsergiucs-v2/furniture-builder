## Context

The user first asked for materials from **Egger, Kastamonu, Fundermax and Kronospan**, in the types **PAL, MDF and
kitchen worktops**, with the furniture drawn in each material's colour and structure. They then narrowed the work to
pictures on materials (ADR-0048). They removed the labels requirement from that work and asked for it to be kept
here as a to-do. Pictures on materials ship without these labels.

## Decision

None yet. This record only keeps the requirement and its open questions until the user decides.

## Alternatives considered

To be weighed when the questions below are answered:
- free-text labels;
- a fixed list of the four manufacturers and three types, plus "Other";
- a catalog of real supplier decors. This last option needs verified supplier data with a cited source, and
  nothing may be invented (ADR-0010).

## Consequences

Until decided:
- materials have a name, thickness, grain, sheet and optional picture only;
- no manufacturer or type is stored, filtered or shown.

## Evidence (repository files)

- `docs/backlog.md` N44 (Not done).
- ADR-0048 lists the labels as deferred.

## Compliance check

`grep -rn "manufacturer" src/core/types.ts` finds nothing until this ADR is decided and built.

## Open questions

1. Are labels free text, or a fixed list (Egger, Kastamonu, Fundermax, Kronospan, Other; PAL, MDF, kitchen
   worktop)?
2. Do labels filter the board choices in the editor and the Materials dialog?
3. Should a kitchen-worktop board be offered only for worktop parts, or anywhere?
4. Should the app ever ship supplier decors (codes, names, thicknesses, colours)? If so, which source does the user
   provide, as ADR-0010 requires?
