## Context

The exploded panel viewer and the positioned assembly editor help users understand a design on a phone. They must
not become a second source of truth for manufacturing data, and must work where WebGL is unavailable.

## Decision

The React-Three-Fiber viewer renders `FurnitureModel` solids (ADR-0018) with faithful mm→m conversion. The
assembly editor stores per-instance integer-mm positions and integer-degree rotations on `Project.assemblies`
(no Dexie schema change) and **never** edits engine part dimensions. `dnd-kit` reordering changes only the order of
existing part IDs. The viewer is lazy-loaded, capped at 40 distinct part rows, and degrades to an accessible list
without WebGL. A 3D view is an exploded/positioned visualisation, **not** an assembly design, structural check or
cut layout.

## Alternatives considered

- **Make the 3D scene the editing model** — rejected: user cut data would depend on a 946 KB optional chunk and on WebGL.

## Consequences

- Collision diagnostics check static panel extents only — no drawer travel, hinge swing, fasteners or loads.
- Any 3D interaction that proposes a dimension change must round-trip through the configurator and validators.

## Compliance check

`reorderParts.ts` returns the same multiset of IDs; `Assembly3DEditor.tsx` never imports `optimize.ts`; `src/tests/unit/reorder.test.ts` and `panel3d-layout.test.ts` pass.
