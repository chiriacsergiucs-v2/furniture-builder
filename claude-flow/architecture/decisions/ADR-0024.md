## Context

All fronts are full overlay today (ADR-0006). Inset fronts sit inside the carcass; half overlay shares a divider
between two fronts. Handles affect the hardware list and sometimes the front (finger pulls).

## Decision

Add a cabinet-level `frontStyle: 'overlay' | 'half' | 'inset'` (default `overlay`, so old designs are unchanged).
The front-zone calculation (ADR-0006) gets one branch per style; gap rules stay exact. Inset fronts sit at
`z ∈ [depth − thickness, depth]` and the envelope check treats them as carcass. Handles become `handle: { kind, length,
position: 'top'|'centre'|'bottom' }` per front-bearing space; they appear in the hardware list with their centre
position on the front, as an annotation. No drilling template, screw length or load claim (ADR-0010).

## Alternatives considered

- **Per-front style** — flexible but rarely used; per-cabinet first, per-space later if needed.

## Consequences

- Inset requires the internal drawer setback and shelf setback to grow by the front thickness; handled in the same
  place as internal drawers.
- Fuzz must cover all three styles.

## Evidence (repository files)

- `src/core/cabinet.ts` — `FrontZone`, door and drawer-front placement.
- `src/core/build.ts` — `FRONT_ROLES` envelope rule.

## Compliance check

When accepted: fuzz asserts exact gaps for every style; overlay designs build identical models to today.
