## Context

Research feature *Consumer order completeness* (value 3.8, P0/P1 by parent *Automatic BOM*; IKEA Kitchen: missing
items escape planning). The verify batch (N45) showed the project Parts CSV, sheets and print already exist. They are
cutting documents: boards by size. What to **buy** is spread over the Parts tab (sheets, edge banding), the hardware
schedule (`hardwareSummary`), run parts, appliance niches ("per its drawing") and user-specified hardware (sliding,
flaps, fixed fronts). Nothing collects them, and nothing says what is still undecided.

## Decision

- **Project and piece → Parts → "Shopping list"** with sections:
  1. *Boards*: per board, number of stock sheets from nesting (ADR-0018), or m² when the board has no sheet size;
  2. *Edge banding*: metres per board and thickness;
  3. *Hardware*: every scheduled item with count and source pieces (hinges, runners, rails, handles, brackets);
  4. *Run parts*: worktop, plinth, fillers, covers (ADR-0036, ADR-0051);
  5. **To choose before buying**: items the model needs but whose product is user-specified — appliance per its
     drawing, sliding/flap hardware (ADR-0037), fixed-front fixing (ADR-0051), safety items awaiting values.
- **Not counted** line, always shown: "Screws, dowels, cams and glue are not counted" — they are not modelled and
  are not invented (ADR-0010).
- **Outputs:** Shopping list CSV (Romanian, `;`, like the Parts CSV) and a print page (ADR-0020). Prices appear per
  line when ADR-0059's price list has them.
- Online ordering is ADR-0060 (Pending).

## Alternatives considered

- **Extend the Parts CSV** — mixes cutting sizes with purchase quantities; cutters and shops need different lists.
- **Count fasteners from joints** — joint fixings are not modelled; any count would be invented.

## Consequences

- "Complete" means: every modelled part and every scheduled item, plus an explicit *to choose* list.
