## Context

Veneered/laminated PAL has a visible grain direction; some parts must follow it. Stock sheets have a finite count
(or `null` for unlimited), damaged edges are trimmed, and some parts may not be rotated even on grainless material.

## Decision

A placement candidate is generated only if: the source material ID matches the part; rotation is attempted only
when both `part.rotationAllowed` and `settings.rotationAllowed` are true and the part is not square; `grainFits`
accepts the part's `grain` against the sheet's `grain` for the chosen orientation; the part fits the usable
rectangle after `trim` (`usableOf`) with kerf (ADR-0008); and sheet/offcut `quantity` is not exceeded. Coordinates
are expressed on the full physical sheet with trimmed margins excluded from the usable region.

## Alternatives considered

- **Treat grain as a soft penalty** — rejected: a grain violation is a scrapped panel, not a cosmetic cost.
- **Trim as a post-process shrink** — rejected: coordinates must map to the physical sheet for the SVG/CSV outputs the operator uses at the saw.

## Consequences

- Offcuts are modelled as sources with zero trim and explicit `quantity ≥ 1`.
- The validator independently recomputes `usable`, grain fit and per-source consumption against `quantity` (ADR-0012).
- New constraints (e.g. machine max cut length) must be added symmetrically to the generator and the validator.

## Compliance check

Kernel tests cover a grain-locked part on a cross-grain sheet (must be unplaced, not rotated) and a quantity-1 sheet that cannot be used twice.
