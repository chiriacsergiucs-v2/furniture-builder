## Context

Counting 54 ready catalogue entries as "most of 90 variants done" would mislead planning: many entries reuse the
same carcass generator with different defaults, while beds, wardrobes with rails/doors, lifts and wall mounting
have no generator at all.

## Decision

B24 remains **Partially done** in the ledger regardless of catalogue size. A family counts as complete only when
every specified mechanism has a real generator, validation and a parity test. Agents report "N ready recipes" and
"M families complete" as two separate numbers and never promote one to the other.

## Alternatives considered

- **Count variants by catalogue entries** — rejected by explicit user rule.

## Consequences

- `delivery/backlog.json` mirrors the ledger's B24 status and the per-family mechanism gaps.
- The next B24 slice is specified in TSK-004/TSK-005 (bed support, wardrobe rails/fronts), not implemented.

## Evidence (repository files)

- `AGENTS.md` — "18-family / 90-variant target remains B24 Partial; 49 ready catalog entries are not 49 fully completed variants of the specified 90 mechanisms."
- `docs/modular-engine-backlog.md` — B24 "Partially done"; `docs/progress.json.nextAction` — "Do not count conceptual 90 variants as ready."

## Compliance check

Ledger row B24 says Partially done; any status change cites a generator file and a parity test for the family claimed.
