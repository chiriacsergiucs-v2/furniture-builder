## Context

Desks, tables, beds and wardrobes are not carcasses with extra panels. Early attempts to force a worktop onto a
cabinet root produced fake side panels in the cutlist.

## Decision

Each family uses the structural root that matches its construction: `core/carcass` for casework, `core/surface`
(`SurfaceSpec`, `initialSurfaceSpecForTemplate`) for worktops/desks/tables with support layouts. Families without a
real generator (bed slat support, wardrobe rails/doors, lifts, wall mounting) stay `planned` (ADR-0020) until a
dedicated root exists with its own validation.

## Alternatives considered

- **One universal "box" root with optional panels** — rejected: generates physically wrong parts and hides missing engineering.

## Consequences

- Adding a family = new root definition + spec type + template entries + parity test, in that order.
- Shared children (shelf, drawer, front, plinth) must remain attachable to any compatible root slot.

## Evidence (repository files)

- `src/furniture/templates.ts` — "Worktop/support designs use a separate structural root, never a fake carcass."; `TemplateKind` includes `surface|desk`.
- `src/furniture/surface.ts`, `src/components/SurfaceConfigurator.tsx`.
- `AGENTS.md` §B24 — "ten desks/tables have a distinct `core/surface` root".

## Compliance check

Every `ready` template has `rootComponent` or `surfaceSpec`; none generates carcass panels for a surface family (parity test `template-expansion.test.ts`).
