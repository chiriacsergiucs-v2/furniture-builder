## Context

Wardrobe (sliding 2-/3-door), credenza (sliding doors), media console (drop-down flap) and wall cabinet (lift-up
flap) variants need fronts that are not hinged doors (ADR-0005 `doors(1|2)`).

## Decision

- `DoorsFront` gains `kind: 'sliding'` (`count` 2–4, `overlap` mm, `track: { depth }`) and `kind: 'flap'`
  (`direction: 'up' | 'down'`). Absent fields keep today's hinged doors.
- **Sliding:** doors span the space opening plus overlaps, in two planes in front of the carcass; the carcass depth
  stays the user's, the model adds the track depth to the bounds; track lengths are scheduled as hardware lines
  ("sliding track — confirm with the system's datasheet"); no hinge lines. Rule (ADR-0035): interior depth for
  hanging is measured behind the track.
- **Flap:** one front over the space with a hardware line "flap stay / lift system — choose by front weight and
  height from the datasheet". No fitting is chosen for the user; the design shows a warning until the user names it.
- Gap arithmetic for flaps is the ADR-0006 overlay gap; sliding doors have no outer reveal.

## Alternatives considered

- **Sliding doors as a separate product root** — rejected: they are fronts on a space, like hinged doors.

## Consequences

- Elevation draws sliding doors with an overlap hatch; 3D places the two planes.

## Evidence (repository files)

- `src/core/types.ts` (`DoorsFront`), `src/core/cabinet.ts` (fronts and hinge scheduling).

## Compliance check

When accepted: tests — sliding doors cover the opening exactly with the stated overlaps, no hinge lines, track
hardware present; a flap schedules its stay line and warns until named; fuzz picks both kinds without overlaps.

## Open questions (defaults used until answered)

1. Sliding door overlap — default 30 mm; track depth default 80 mm (both editable).

## Implementation (2026-10-23)

Built as decided with the defaults (overlap 30, track 80). Sliding doors are two planes at the carcass front and
`tf + 2` mm in front of it; the cabinet envelope check allows the track depth. Under the inset front style sliding
doors take the inset front zone (so gaps line up with neighbouring fronts) and a warning says inset does not apply.
Flaps schedule a stay line and warn until the fitting is named. The inspector's *Front* control offers None / Door /
Pair / Sliding / Flap.

Evidence: `src/core/types.ts` (`SlidingFront`, `FlapFront`), `src/core/cabinet.ts`, `src/core/design.ts`
(`validFront`), `src/ui/Editors.tsx` (`FrontEditor`); `tests/constructions.test.ts` (sliding and flap block),
fuzz (sliding planes, reveal and runner checks).
