## Context

Drawer runners, hinges, handles and back-panel fixings are purchased, not cut. Early mock-ups listed them among
panels and corrupted sheet statistics.

## Decision

Hardware is modelled as `HardwareRequirement` entries (type, quantity, nominal size) on the `FurnitureModel`, listed
separately in the UI. It never enters `Project.parts`, the optimiser or the cutlist. The schedule is a *count* of
required sets, not a drilling pattern or supplier BOM, and the UI says so.

## Alternatives considered

- **Represent hardware as zero-area parts** — rejected: pollutes validation and exports.

## Consequences

- Door/back/plinth components "require hinge and back attachment sets without claiming fitting specifications".
- Supplier part numbers and drilling patterns are B26 (deferred) and need physical validation (TSK-007).

## Compliance check

No `Part` with a material of type hardware exists; `HardwareRequirement` is not referenced from `src/engine`.
