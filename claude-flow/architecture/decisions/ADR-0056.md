## Context

Research feature *Construct the floor plan in 2D* (value 3.5, P0 by parent; HomeByMe, IKEA PAX room-modelling
complaints). ADR-0055 gives a rectangular room. Real kitchens and bedrooms often have an L shape, a chimney breast or
an alcove; users need to draw that outline on the plan, not type a rectangle.

## Decision

- **Room outline** = a closed list of corner points in whole mm where every wall is parallel to x or z
  (`room.outline: [x, z][]`, optional; absent = ADR-0055's rectangle). Walls carry ids so ADR-0055 features attach to
  a wall id instead of `'back' | 'left' | …`.
- **Drawing on the plan:** Project → Room → **Draw the room**: tap corners on the plan; each new wall snaps to
  horizontal or vertical and to whole 10 mm; *Close* joins the last corner to the first. Every wall length is then
  editable as a mm field (the list of walls with lengths), so precise numbers always win.
- **Templates:** *Rectangle*, *L-shape*, *U-shape*, *Alcove* fill the outline from four to six lengths.
- **Checks:** ADR-0055's `outside-room` uses the outline (a piece must lie inside it). A self-crossing or open outline
  is an error on the room ("walls cross at …") and room checks are skipped until fixed (ADR-0008).
- Each wall edit is one undo step (ADR-0050).

## Alternatives considered

- **Free-angle walls** — break ADR-0004; angled walls stay out until a shaped-wall ADR.
- **Import a floor-plan image to trace** — useful later; tracing still needs this outline model first.

## Consequences

- Pieces still stand against walls by their numbers; snapping a piece to a wall is not part of this ADR.

## Questions and answers (2026-10-24; recommended answers auto-accepted on the user's instruction)

1. *Allow angled walls?* — **No; walls parallel to x or z only.**
2. *Draw by tapping or by typing lengths?* — **Both: tap to sketch, every length editable as mm.**
3. *Snap step while drawing?* — **10 mm (lengths can be typed to 1 mm).**
4. *Offer ready shapes?* — **Yes: Rectangle, L, U, Alcove.**
