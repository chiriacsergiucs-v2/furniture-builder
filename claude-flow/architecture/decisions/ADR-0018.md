## Context

Before B17 the 3D preview, the cut rows and the Fusion script each derived geometry separately and disagreed.

## Decision

`deriveFurnitureModel(project, assembly)` produces `FurnitureSolid[]` (positioned, oriented physical extents),
`ManufacturingRow[]` (actual quantities), `HardwareRequirement[]` and diagnostics. The assembled 3D renderer,
the Fusion snapshot and manufacturing counts all consume this single projection. Cross-template parity tests
(B28) assert that cutlist dimensions equal model extents for every `ready` recipe and that no panels overlap.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep per-consumer derivations with shared helpers** — rejected: drift returned within one iteration.

## Consequences

- Any output that shows furniture geometry must consume `FurnitureModel`; a new consumer that re-derives is a defect.
- The model certifies geometric consistency only — not joinery, strength, drilling or hardware suitability (ADR-0019, ADR-0024).

## Evidence (repository files)

- `src/furniture/furniture-model.ts` — "One normalized view of the saved assembly for 3D, Fusion snapshot and manufacturing counts. This does not certify joint engineering, strength, drilling or hardware suitability."
- `src/furniture/geometry-diagnostics.ts` — intersecting-extent rejection.
- `src/tests/unit/furniture-model.test.ts` (B28).
- `src/components/PositionedFurniturePreview.tsx`, `src/assembly/fusion.ts`.

## Compliance check

`grep -rn "deriveFurnitureModel" src/` lists the 3D preview, Fusion export and manufacturing UI as callers; parity test passes for all ready templates.
