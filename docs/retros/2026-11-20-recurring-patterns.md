# Retro 2026-11-20 — recurring patterns across ADRs, gaps, PRs and CI

Scope: the whole history of the repository up to `origin/main` 6a5720cd (2026-11-20): 1389 commits, 300 pull requests,
1104 runs of `ci.yml` (182 failed), 279 implemented ADRs, 5 pending ADRs, 153 open gaps, 320 archived gaps, the backlog,
the 8 earlier retros and the research audits. Analysis only: no rule, hook, skill or app code was changed.

Method: incidents were collected from each source (ADRs and ledgers; CLAUDE.md, skills, hooks, retros and git log; pull
requests and CI runs), then grouped. A pattern needs at least 3 incidents from at least 2 source types; weaker clusters
are under _Watchlist_. Key claims were re-checked against `git log`, the files and the CI run list in this session;
counts that come from a full scan are marked as scan counts.

Column values: **Category** — repetitive action, process slip, rework loop, critical mistake, tooling. **Severity** —
critical (a shipped bug, lost data or a broken hard rule), high (rework across 2 or more PRs), medium, low. **Cost to
fix** — S/M/L. **Did it work?** — yes, partly, no, too new (fewer than 3 PRs since it was added).

## Summary — top 5

1. **P3 Main goes red after a merge** — 8 episodes, 6 PRs merged on a red head; the safeguard was removed a day after it
   was added.
2. **P1 Id collisions between parallel branches** — 47 "renumber" commits, 4 duplicate ids still in the backlog, and
   one collision reached `main` after the new hook and CI check existed.
3. **P4 "Done" without evidence** — 42 Done rows whose own evidence says unmerged, 38 unchecked Done-when items in 23
   shipped ADRs.
4. **P7 Countermeasures removed or churned before the problem was fixed** — one revert removed 5 safeguards, and 6
   problems came back; CLAUDE.md changed 83 times in four weeks.
5. **P2 Checks skipped locally fail in CI** — 10 lint and 10 format fix-up commits, 20 format-failure runs, 5
   guard-test failures.
